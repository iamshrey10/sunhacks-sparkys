# Cool Route — Requirements

Source of truth: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/API_CONTRACT.md` (with
`backend/api/schemas.py` as the contract in code). Where this document and those
disagree, the docs win.

## Product summary

Cool Route is a mobile-first app (Android APK via Capacitor plus a hosted web
build) that finds the lowest-sun walking route across ASU Tempe and Mill Ave,
shows it beside the fastest route, and surfaces the nearest open water or
cooling spot through a "Help now" screen. Deadline: working demo in under
8 hours, four developers, one module each. No database, no sign-in.

## Honesty constraints (apply to every requirement)

These are hard constraints, not features. They override any UI or routing
behavior that would conflict.

- **Shade is an estimate from map data.** Sun exposure is computed from building
  footprints, tree tags and covered-walkway data, not measured. The app never
  claims to measure temperature.
- **Unknown hours show as "hours unknown."** `open_now: null` is always rendered
  as "hours unknown"; the app never guesses a resource is open or closed.
- **Sample data is always labelled.** When mocks are on (`VITE_USE_MOCKS=true`)
  a visible "SAMPLE DATA" badge is shown. The API never returns
  `source: "sample"`; sample JSON is never presented as a real result.
- **No medical advice.** Help-now content is general safety information only, no
  diagnosis or treatment guidance. Any red-flag emergency guidance is decided by
  fixed code rules, never generated by a model.

## Requirement 1 — Coolest vs fastest route

**User story:** As a pedestrian in Tempe, I want to see the coolest walking route
next to the fastest one, so that I can choose how much extra walking I trade for
shade.

### Acceptance criteria

1. WHEN the user submits a start, end and leave-at time THEN the system SHALL call
   `POST /route` with a valid `RouteRequest` and render both the `fastest` and
   `coolest` `RouteOption`s on one map.
2. THE map SHALL draw the fastest route in grey and the coolest route in blue,
   with OpenStreetMap attribution visible.
3. THE comparison SHALL show, for each route, extra minutes versus fastest,
   `shade_pct` (0–100), and sun-exposed minutes (from `sun_exposed_s`), using only
   values present in the `RouteResponse`.
4. WHEN the coolest route is not meaningfully cooler than the fastest THEN the
   comparison SHALL say so rather than implying a benefit.
5. A shade-weight slider SHALL let the user re-request with a `shade_weight`
   between 0 (fastest) and 1 (coolest regardless of length); the map and
   comparison SHALL update to the new response.
6. THE displayed numbers SHALL come from the `RouteResponse` only; the UI SHALL NOT
   compute or invent route metrics.

## Requirement 2 — Avoid stairs

**User story:** As someone who cannot easily use stairs, I want to exclude stairs
from my route, so that the suggested path is actually walkable for me.

### Acceptance criteria

1. THE route form SHALL expose an "avoid stairs" toggle that sets
   `prefs.avoid_stairs` in the `RouteRequest`.
2. WHEN `avoid_stairs` is true THEN the route finder SHALL exclude `highway=steps`
   segments from the returned routes.
3. THE returned `RouteOption.stairs_count` SHALL reflect stairs actually on the
   route (0 when stairs are excluded, except where the contract's data cannot
   avoid them — see the design's contract-gap note).

## Requirement 3 — Leave-at time

**User story:** As a user planning a walk later today, I want to pick when I leave,
so that the shade estimate matches the sun position at that time.

### Acceptance criteria

1. THE route form SHALL provide a leave-at time picker producing a `depart_time`
   in `HH:MM` local Tempe time (America/Phoenix, no daylight saving).
2. THE route finder SHALL snap `depart_time` to the nearest precomputed shade
   layer (09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00) and return which layer was used in
   `shade_layer`.
3. THE response's `depart_time` SHALL echo the requested time and the UI MAY show
   the snapped `shade_layer` so the estimate basis is clear.

## Requirement 4 — Plain-English request

**User story:** As a user who does not want to fill a form, I want to type my
request in plain English, so that the app fills the settings for me.

### Acceptance criteria

1. THE app SHALL provide a text box that sends the user's text (1–500 chars) to
   `POST /parse`.
2. THE parse response SHALL be used to fill the form: `depart_time` (or left
   blank when null), `destination_text` (or blank when null) and `prefs`.
3. WHEN the input is unclear THEN parsing SHALL return nulls rather than invented
   values; the form SHALL NOT be filled with guessed times or destinations.
4. AFTER a route result is shown THE app MAY call `POST /explain` and display its
   `text` under the comparison; the explanation SHALL cite only numbers present in
   the route result.
5. WHEN `/parse` or `/explain` returns `501` or fails THEN the app SHALL remain
   usable through the manual form (graceful degradation).

## Requirement 5 — Help now (nearest water or cooling spot)

**User story:** As someone overheating outdoors, I want one button that shows the
nearest open water or cooling spot, so that I can get relief quickly.

### Acceptance criteria

1. THE "Help now" screen SHALL call `GET /resources?lat&lng&radius_m` for the
   user's location and show the resources on a map and in a list, sorted by
   distance.
2. EACH resource SHALL show an "open now", "closed" or "hours unknown" badge
   derived from `open_now` (`true` / `false` / `null` respectively); `null` SHALL
   always render as "hours unknown".
3. WHERE available, each resource SHALL show its `source_url` and `last_checked`
   so the user can judge how current the hours are.
4. TAPPING a resource SHALL let the user route to it (reusing the route flow).
5. THE screen SHALL show general safety information only and SHALL NOT give
   medical advice; any emergency red-flag guidance SHALL come from fixed code
   rules, not a model.

## Requirement 6 — Saved preferences

**User story:** As a returning user, I want my preferences remembered, so that I
do not re-enter them every time.

### Acceptance criteria

1. THE app SHALL persist `Prefs` (`avoid_stairs`, `heat_sensitivity`,
   `shade_weight`) in phone local storage; there is no database or sign-in.
2. WHEN the app reloads THEN saved preferences SHALL be restored into the form.
3. ALL local-storage access SHALL be wrapped so a storage failure degrades to
   defaults rather than crashing, with loading, empty and error states handled.

## Out of scope

Only the six requirements above are in scope. No temperature measurement, no
indoor-heat or homelessness features, no accounts, no database, and no features
beyond this list.
